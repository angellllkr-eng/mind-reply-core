## Purpose
Define the evidence required before NOVA processes real player personal data. The current Digital Hall remains synthetic/demo-first.

## Processing inventory to complete before production
- Account registration and authentication
- Identity/KYC information
- Payment and transaction information
- Player activity and responsible-gambling signals
- Support/complaint records
- Marketing preferences and consent records
- Security/audit logs
- Cookies, analytics and device identifiers
- Supplier/provider data exchanges
- Staff/admin access records

For each processing activity record: purpose, lawful basis, categories of data, data subjects, recipients, international transfers, retention, deletion method, security controls, rights process, processor/DPA reference, DPIA requirement and owner.

## Security and breach control
Personal-data incidents must be triaged, documented and handled against the applicable GDPR/national requirements. The EDPB notes that organisations must document and handle breaches appropriately and identifies circumstances requiring supervisory-authority notification within 72 hours. [PRIMARY SOURCE: EDPB personal data breaches / Guidelines 9/2022]

## Production gate
No real customer dataset, KYC record, payment credential, or sensitive player profile is introduced into DEMO. STAGING uses synthetic identities unless an approved test-data process explicitly permits otherwise. Production requires approved privacy documentation, processor contracts, retention rules, access controls, incident response, and market-specific legal review.
