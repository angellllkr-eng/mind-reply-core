## Supplier packet
For each game/live/payment/KYC/security supplier collect and verify:
- Legal entity and contracting entity
- Ownership/contact information
- Relevant licences/approvals and exact scope
- Market-by-market availability
- Contract and termination rights
- API/RGS/live-stream architecture
- Sandbox/test environment
- Security documentation and penetration-test posture
- Data-processing terms and subprocessors
- Incident/SLA/support commitments
- Game/RNG/testing/certification evidence where applicable
- Reporting/session-data schema
- Responsible-gambling and market-access capabilities
- Branding/customisation rights
- Business continuity and exit plan
- Pricing/minimums/revenue share

## Decision rule
Supplier marketing is REFERENCE_ONLY. Contracts, regulator records, technical documentation, security evidence and direct confirmations become evidence only after validation and date stamping.

## Provider neutrality
Evolution, Pragmatic Play, Playtech and other suppliers remain candidates until NOVA completes the same diligence packet for each. No provider is represented as selected, contracted or production-integrated by this register.
