# NOVA Formal Governance Charter

Status: CONTROLLED DRAFT — NOVA ONLY

## Organisation and authority

Product: NOVA Gaming / NOVA Digital Hall.
Repository: angellllkr-eng/novagaming.
Owner authority: A11ceo.
Operational review layer: HALO AI.
External professional authority: qualified legal counsel, compliance specialists, security assessors, regulators, licensed suppliers and payment/KYC providers where applicable.

A11ceo is the product-owner authority for scope, release approval, commercial direction and irreversible decisions. HALO AI is a review and assurance layer: it may inspect evidence, identify gaps, prepare findings and recommend remediation, but it must not represent itself as a regulator, lawyer, auditor, licensed operator, supplier, or human decision-maker.

No AI agent may convert a plan, benchmark, generated artifact, mock integration, public marketing claim or unsigned document into evidence of licensing, certification, contract, security assurance or production approval.

## Formal decision classes

OWNER_APPROVAL: required for production release, regulated-market activation, real-money functionality, contracts, external commitments, destructive changes and material policy changes.

CONTROLLED_ENGINEERING: may be executed through reviewed repository changes, automated checks and reversible deployments.

EVIDENCE_REVIEW: factual verification against regulator, supplier, legal, security and deployment sources.

REFERENCE_ONLY: useful research or historical material that cannot itself authorize a release.

## Release principle

NOVA is released only when the evidence pack matches the actual product state. If evidence is missing, the relevant capability remains disabled or clearly marked as unavailable.

The standard is not 'nothing can ever go wrong'. The standard is controlled operation: truthful claims, least privilege, tested changes, observable failures, documented rollback, current legal/compliance evidence and a traceable release record.
