## Purpose
This register separates jurisdiction research from NOVA's own evidence. A supplier licence, general EU presence, or historical plan never establishes NOVA market access.

## Control rule
A market remains DISABLED until legal counsel/compliance confirms the target entity, licence or exemption, product classification, consumer-access rule, advertising requirements, age controls, responsible-gambling duties, payment/KYC/AML requirements, technical testing, reporting, and deployment geography applicable to that market.

## Current research baseline
- Great Britain: the UK Gambling Commission states that a business providing remote gambling facilities to GB consumers needs the relevant remote licence; remote licence holders must comply with RTS and applicable testing requirements. [PRIMARY SOURCE: UKGC remote sector and remote casino licence pages]
- European Union: gambling regulation remains materially national/jurisdiction-specific. EU-level membership or a supplier's EU presence does not create a universal NOVA operating authorisation.
- Bulgaria and every other intended market require a separate current legal/compliance determination before activation.

## Required evidence fields per market
1. Target country/territory
2. Intended product classification
3. Target consumer population
4. Operating entity and beneficial ownership evidence
5. Required operator/software/host/supplier licences
6. Local market-access rule
7. Age/identity controls
8. Responsible-gambling requirements
9. Advertising/affiliate restrictions
10. KYC/AML and sanctions requirements
11. Payment and customer-funds requirements
12. Technical/game/RNG testing
13. Security and incident requirements
14. Data-protection/privacy requirements
15. Consumer terms/complaints requirements
16. Language/currency/localisation requirements
17. Reporting/record-retention requirements
18. Regulator/authority source URLs
19. Counsel/compliance sign-off reference
20. Evidence expiry/review date

## Release states
RESEARCHED → COUNSEL_REVIEW → EVIDENCE_COMPLETE → OWNER_APPROVED → ENABLED.
Any stale, missing, contradictory, or expired evidence returns the market to DISABLED.

## Forecast / drift controls
Monitor regulator rule changes, fee changes, technical-standard revisions, enforcement guidance, supplier market availability, payment-provider terms, and data-protection guidance. Forecasts are planning signals only and never substitute for current legal evidence.
