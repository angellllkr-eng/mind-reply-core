## Localization requirements

- Translation keys, not embedded copy.
- Legal text versioned by jurisdiction.
- Date/time/currency formatting locale-aware.
- Number formatting tested for decimal separators.
- Responsible-gambling terminology reviewed by local counsel/translator.
- Search/category taxonomy localized independently from legal content.
- Accessibility labels translated and tested.
- RTL readiness preserved even if not currently required.
