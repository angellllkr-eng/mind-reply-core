# NOVA — Research Tool Boundary

NOVA is an independent project.

Research/design/engineering tools may support research, validation, visualization, prototyping or implementation, but they do not become NOVA dependencies merely because they were used during research.

## Research workflow

1. Primary regulator / official source.
2. Specialist research and methodology.
3. Firecrawl/source extraction for evidence collection.
4. Product/UX comparison.
5. Visual/interaction synthesis.
6. Engineering implementation in NOVA.
7. Automated test.
8. Evidence record.

## Evidence rule

A tool output is research evidence only when its source, date, scope and interpretation are recorded. A generated design, benchmark, model response or visualization is not a regulatory fact.

## Multi-model rule

Different models may independently analyze the same requirement. Conflicts are resolved against primary authoritative sources, not majority vote.

## Voice / router rule

Voice, routing and orchestration layers are optional interfaces. They must never bypass jurisdiction, KYC, responsible-gambling, payment, authorization or production activation gates.

No external platform is assumed to be part of NOVA.
