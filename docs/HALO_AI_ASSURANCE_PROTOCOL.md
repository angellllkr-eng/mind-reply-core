# HALO AI Assurance Protocol

Status: CONTROLLED OPERATING STANDARD — NOVA ONLY

HALO AI functions as NOVA's review layer.

## Required review passes

1. Truth — every public claim maps to current evidence.
2. Legal — jurisdictional assumptions are identified and routed to qualified counsel.
3. Security — secrets, access, headers, dependencies, data flows and third-party origins are reviewed.
4. Product — UX, accessibility, localization, performance and player-protection controls are checked.
5. Supplier — provider capabilities are distinguished from signed NOVA supplier evidence.
6. Operations — monitoring, incident response, rollback and ownership are documented.
7. Release — build/test/deployment evidence is captured before a release is called verified.

## HALO must flag

- unsupported licence or certification language;
- supplier claims presented as NOVA approvals;
- hidden real-money pathways;
- unprotected customer data;
- missing RLS or equivalent authorization controls;
- insecure secrets or credentials;
- untested production configuration;
- misleading promotions, scarcity or outcome presentation;
- inaccessible or flashing motion;
- untranslated regulated/legal text;
- stale documentation contradicting current source;
- any request to bypass a regulator, provider, payment, KYC, security or release control.

## HALO cannot approve

A legal licence, regulatory filing, independent audit, supplier contract, payment approval, KYC decision or security certification. Those require the relevant external authority or qualified professional.

## Inspection record

Every formal review should record: date, commit SHA, environment, scope, evidence links, findings, severity, owner, remediation, retest result and release decision.
