## Severity
SEV-1: player safety, funds, authentication compromise, major data breach, or regulatory-critical failure.
SEV-2: material service/security degradation without confirmed critical compromise.
SEV-3: limited defect or isolated operational issue.
SEV-4: informational/minor issue.

## Response sequence
1. DETECT — record timestamp, environment, commit and alert source.
2. CONTAIN — disable affected route, market, provider or capability where necessary.
3. PRESERVE — retain relevant logs/evidence without exposing unnecessary personal data.
4. ASSESS — security, privacy, player-safety, funds and regulatory impact.
5. NOTIFY — follow the approved internal and external notification matrix.
6. RECOVER — restore from known-good state or rollback.
7. VERIFY — smoke-test and inspect affected controls.
8. RECORD — incident report, root cause, evidence, decisions and follow-up actions.
9. LEARN — corrective action, regression test and control update.

## Emergency authority
A11ceo may stop release or production capability. HALO AI may flag and document risk but does not replace legal counsel, regulators, security assessors, payment providers, KYC providers or human operational authority.

## Data breach note
For personal-data incidents, apply the current GDPR/national breach process. EDPB guidance identifies documentation and notification considerations, including the 72-hour supervisory-authority rule where applicable.
