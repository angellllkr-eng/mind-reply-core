# NOVA Provider Landscape — UK/EU

Status: RESEARCHED, NOT CONTRACTED
Scope: NOVA only. This is a supplier-selection evidence map, not a production approval.

## What matters

NOVA should evaluate providers against the same evidence set:
- UK and target-EU market availability and supplier approvals
- live casino, RNG/slots, table and game-show coverage
- integration model and operator control
- multilingual/local-market support
- technical performance and streaming requirements for live
- game/RNG testing and audit evidence
- commercial terms, exclusivity, minimums and launch support
- responsible-gambling and market-access controls
- branding/custom-table options
- exit/portability and data/reporting requirements

A provider's licence or market presence does not license NOVA. NOVA's own operator status, market access, contracts and controls remain separate.

## Current evidence

### Evolution
Evolution publicly lists regulated supplier licences/approvals across multiple markets, including the UK, Malta, Bulgaria, Romania, Spain, Denmark and Sweden. Its current solutions material describes a single integration option, dedicated branded live environments and promotion tooling. Its public live portfolio also states support across 15 languages and more than 2,000 live tables. These are supplier-side capabilities; commercial availability to NOVA must still be confirmed in diligence.

### Pragmatic Play
Pragmatic Play's current Live Casino page states UK and MGA licences and certifications for several European markets including Italy, Sweden, Denmark, Romania and Bulgaria. It describes a broad live portfolio and operator-facing risk-management/support capabilities. Its historical MGA material states its portfolio was available to operator partners through a single API. Current commercial/API terms still require direct supplier confirmation.

### Playtech
Playtech's current Live Casino page describes European, US and LatAm studios, bespoke content, operator partnerships and work in regulated markets. Playtech also publicly states it is licensed/regulated in Great Britain and holds a Malta critical gaming supply corporate licence. Current commercial scope, market-by-market availability and technical integration terms require supplier confirmation.

## Selection rule

Do not pick a provider from marketing claims alone. The next diligence packet should request:
1. market-by-market approval matrix for NOVA's intended launch countries;
2. B2B/B2C licensing responsibilities;
3. API/RGS/live-stream technical documentation;
4. game and RNG testing/certification package;
5. incident, uptime and support SLAs;
6. data-processing/security documentation;
7. reporting and player-session data fields;
8. pricing, revenue share/minimums and termination terms;
9. branding/customisation options;
10. sandbox/test credentials only after commercial and security review.

## Current focus

For the near-term NOVA build, keep the app provider-neutral. Build the lobby, provider catalogue, live-floor shell, game metadata model, language layer and security boundaries so a selected supplier can be integrated without redesigning the player experience.
